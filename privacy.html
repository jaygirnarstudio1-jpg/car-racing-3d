Privacy Policy

Car Racing 3D Game

📌 Last Updated:

Welcome to Car Racing 3D ("we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you play our game. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our practices.

1. Information We Collect

Our game does not collect any personal information from users. Specifically:

  • No Personal Data: We do not collect your name, email, phone number, or address.
  • No Account Required: You can play without creating any account.
  • No Camera/Microphone: The game does not access your device's camera or microphone.
  • No Location Data: We do not track your GPS or location.
  • No Contact Access: The game does not access your contacts or files.

2. Local Storage

Our game uses your browser's Local Storage to save your game progress. This includes:

  • Your high score
  • Game settings (such as sound on/off)

This data is stored only on your device and is never sent to any server. You can clear this data at any time by clearing your browser data.

3. Third-Party Services

Our game does not use any third-party analytics, advertising networks, or tracking services.

4. Children's Privacy

Our game is safe for all ages, including children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children. Since we collect no data at all, there is no risk to children's privacy.

5. Data Security

Since we do not collect or transmit any personal data, there is no risk of data breach. All game data is stored locally on your device.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

7. Your Rights

You have full control over your data:

  • You can clear your game data anytime via browser settings.
  • You can uninstall or delete the game at any time.
  • No data is stored on our servers, so there is nothing to request or delete from us.
